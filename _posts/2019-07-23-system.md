# Zheng’s work system in 2019

## What should I do?

1. Meta work: think about your long term **goals** (e.g. defend by 2020.10).
2. Divide them into smaller **projects** (e.g. writing and revising Chapter 2,3,4,5,1. Not a typo, intro chapters should be worked on the last).
3. Divide projects into **actionable tasks** (reading stuff for Chapter 2, write section 2.1, etc.)
4. Put all these things in a **task management software** (I use [todoist](https://todoist.com), it’s free.)
5. Use your **calendar** to block times of the day to focus on one task, so you know what to do before the day comes. ([todoist](https://todoist.com) can be linked to your calendar, so you can add stuff on todoist which appears on your calendar, and vice versa.)

## Where did the time go?

1. **Track your time**, it can be all time, wake time, work time, writing time, time for one project. (I use [Toggl](https://toggl.com), it’s free. Available on web, iOS, MacOS, probably other platforms.)
2. Set **focus time**, when starting to work on one task, start a timer of 20/30/40 mins. DO NOT STOP working on this task before the timer stops. When the timer stops, rest for 10 mins, repeat.
3. As said in point 5, use your **calendar** to fill your day.

## But what about instagram?

1. Set your phone to **Do not disturb** (I do it 24/7), put it in somewhere out of your reach.
2. Set app **limits** on your iPhones to restrict access to apps and websites, or better yet, delete the distracting apps.
3. **Block** the access to certain websites during the time you want to focus (I use [simple blocker](https://simpleblocker.com) on the browser, you can also set a timer)
4. If simple blocker doesn’t work because you can switch the block off with a click, use [SelfControl](https://selfcontrolapp.com/) (for MacOS), which will block the websites on any browser, you cannot switch it off without big hassle.

## Others

1. I also try to **switch my location** throughout the day, I feel that it renews my focus.
2. I don’t work at **home**, except for some light reading (actual books, papers for reading groups), I can’t.
3. I try to **use one app for one function**. Definitely don’t use multiple apps for one function, I wouldn’t be able to find the file. I also don’t use one app for multiple functions. I can be distracted by other functions. (e.g. [Notes.app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205773)) for temporary notes, [Bear](https://bear.app/) for longer non-academic writing (like this), [VoodooPad](https://www.voodoopad.com/) for notes for papers, [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/my-drive) for shared notes, [Simplenote](https://simplenote.com/) for a side non-academic side project, [Evernote](https://evernote.com/) for everything else.)
